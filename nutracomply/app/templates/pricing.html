Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about RegBite pricing.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan later? +
Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade at any time from account settings. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades at the next billing cycle.
Is there a free trial for paid plans? +
Yes. The Growth plan comes with a 14-day free trial — no credit card required.
Do you support GST billing for Indian businesses? +
All invoices are GST-compliant with HSN codes. Add your GSTIN to your profile and it will appear on all future invoices for ITC claims.
What payment methods are accepted? +
Credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), UPI, net banking, and bank transfers for Enterprise. All payments processed via Razorpay.
