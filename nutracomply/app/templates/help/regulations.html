{# Regulations feed #}

Regulations feed

The Regulations page shows a chronological feed of detected regulation changes, grouped by month. RegBite's daily scheduler scrapes FSSAI, AYUSH, and eGazette sources at 6:00 AM IST to detect new or updated regulatory documents.

Each entry shows the document name, change type, severity, detection date, and a summary of what changed.

{# Change types #}

Change types

  • NEW — A completely new regulation or amendment detected
  • AMENDMENT — An existing regulation has been modified
  • REPEAL — A regulation has been repealed or superseded
  • CLARIFICATION — An official clarification or order issued
  • UNKNOWN — Change type could not be automatically classified
{# Regulation status #}

Regulation status

Each regulation has a status indicating its current state:

  • DRAFT — Published for public comment, not yet enforceable
  • FINAL — Finalised but not yet in effect (pending effective date)
  • EFFECTIVE — Currently in force and being checked by the compliance engine
  • SUPERSEDED — Replaced by a newer version
{# Auto-recheck #}

Automatic re-checks

When a CRITICAL regulation change is detected, RegBite automatically re-checks all current product labels against the updated rules. If a previously passing label now fails, you'll receive an alert and email notification with the specific new violations. The LLM Knowledge Base is also automatically re-seeded with new regulation data.

{# Reg alerts #}

Regulation Alerts (Reg. Alerts)

The Reg. Alerts page shows curated regulatory alerts published by the RegBite admin team. These are manually reviewed summaries of important regulation changes with severity ratings and actionable guidance. They complement the automated regulation feed with human-verified analysis.

{# Email notifications #}
Stay informed: Configure notification emails in Settings to receive alerts about regulation changes and compliance impacts. The daily scheduler sends email notifications for every new regulation detected and for any label violations triggered by regulation updates.
