{# Overview #}

How the engine works

When you upload a label image, RegBite's AI extracts structured data and runs it through the compliance engine. Each rule performs a specific check — presence of required fields, format validation, ingredient list verification, banned substance detection, and more.

Results are categorised as Pass, Fail, Warning, or Skipped. Every failure includes a specific remediation message explaining what needs to change.

{# Frameworks #}

Regulatory frameworks

FSSAI

FSS Health Supplements Regulations 2022, FSS Labelling & Display Regulations 2020 (Version VIII, Sept 2025), FSS Food Products Standards & Additives First Amendment 2025, FSSAI Licensing Amendment 2026. Covers ingredient approval, nutritional claims, labelling requirements, additive limits, and licensing.

Legal Metrology

Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules 2011. Covers net quantity declarations, MRP display, manufacturer address, date markings, and country of origin.

AYUSH

Ayurvedic, Siddha & Unani (ASU) Drug regulations. Only applies to Ayurvedic/Siddha/Unani category products. Covers ASU license requirements, classical formulation validation, heavy metal testing, and traditional claims.

BIS

Bureau of Indian Standards requirements. Covers ISI mark requirement for applicable categories and protein quality testing standards (IS 7219).

DGFT

Directorate General of Foreign Trade import compliance. Only applies to imported products (non-Indian country of origin). Covers IEC (Import Export Code) requirement and CITES botanical restrictions.

{# Severity levels #}

Severity levels

CRITICAL Must fix immediately. Regulatory violation that could result in product recall, seizure, or legal action. HIGH Important compliance gap. Should be resolved before market release or next batch production. MEDIUM Recommended improvement for full regulatory compliance. May not block market release. LOW Minor suggestion or industry best practice. Not a regulatory requirement.
{# Category filtering #}

Category-based filtering

Not all rules apply to every product. RegBite automatically filters rules based on your product's category:

  • AYUSH rules — Only checked for Ayurvedic, Siddha, and Unani products
  • DGFT import rules — Only checked when country of origin is non-Indian
  • FSSAI + Legal Metrology rules — Applied to all product categories

Rules that don't apply to your product are marked as SKIPPED in the report — they don't affect your compliance score.

{# Manual checker #}

Manual Checker

The Checker tool (in the sidebar) lets you run a quick compliance check by entering product data manually — without uploading a label image. This is useful for pre-production label review or when you want to test specific field combinations.

{# Compliance score #}

Understanding your compliance score

The compliance score is a weighted percentage. Critical rule failures carry more weight than low-severity issues. A score of 100% means all applicable rules passed. Products with any CRITICAL failure are flagged regardless of the overall percentage. The score updates automatically when you re-analyse a label or when rules are updated after a regulation change.
