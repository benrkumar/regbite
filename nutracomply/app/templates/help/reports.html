{# Generating reports #}

Generating a report

Reports are generated automatically after every label compliance check. You can also generate a report manually from any product's label version by clicking "Generate Report".

Each report includes:

  • Overall compliance score (weighted percentage)
  • Pass / Fail / Warning / Skipped breakdown
  • Detailed results for every applicable rule
  • Specific remediation guidance for each failure
  • Extracted label data summary
  • Report reference number for audit trail
{# Downloading #}

Downloading as PDF

Click the "Download" button on any report to get a PDF version suitable for printing or sharing with regulatory consultants. The PDF includes all compliance details and can optionally include your custom branding (logo name and accent colour — configurable in Settings → Report Branding).

{# Sharing #}

Sharing reports

Click "Share" on any report to generate a public link. Anyone with the link can view the report without logging in — useful for sharing with external auditors, regulatory consultants, or supply chain partners.

Share links include a unique token and expire after 30 days by default. You'll receive an email confirmation when a share link is created.

{# Report branding #}

Custom report branding

On Growth and Enterprise plans, you can customise reports with your brand name and accent colour. Go to Settings → Report Branding to set your brand name and choose a hex colour. These appear in the PDF header and on shared report pages.

{# Reports list #}

Reports list

The Reports page in the sidebar shows all your generated reports sorted by date. Each entry shows the product name, compliance score, report reference, and creation date. Click any report to view the full details.
