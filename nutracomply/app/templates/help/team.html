{# Overview #}

Overview

Team management is available to Account Admins and Super Admins. You can invite colleagues, regulatory consultants, or auditors to collaborate on your compliance portfolio. Each team member gets their own login and role-based access.

{# Roles #}

Available roles

Account Admin

Full control over the account — create products, upload labels, manage team members, handle billing, configure settings. This is the default role for the account owner.

Editor

Can create products, upload label images, run compliance checks, generate reports, track regulations, and manage license renewals. Cannot manage team members or billing. Ideal for QA teams and regulatory affairs staff.

Viewer

Read-only access to products, compliance reports, alerts, and regulations. Cannot create products, upload labels, or modify any data. Ideal for stakeholders, management, or external reviewers who need visibility without edit access.

Consultant

External auditor access — can view compliance reports, the regulations feed, and use the compliance checker. Cannot access products directly, manage settings, or see billing information. Ideal for regulatory consultants and third-party auditors.

{# Inviting members #}

Inviting team members

  1. Go to Team in the sidebar
  2. Enter the invitee's email address
  3. Select their role (Editor, Viewer, or Consultant)
  4. Click "Send Invite"
  5. The invitee receives an email with a link valid for 7 days
  6. Once they accept and set a password, they can log in immediately
