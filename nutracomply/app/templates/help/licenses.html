{# Overview #}

Overview

The License Renewal Tracker helps you manage all regulatory licenses required for your food business. Add your licenses, track expiry dates, and receive automatic email reminders 30 days before expiry.

{# License types #}

Supported license types

  • FSSAI Registration — For businesses with annual turnover up to ₹12 lakh
  • FSSAI State License — For turnover between ₹12 lakh and ₹20 crore
  • FSSAI Central License — For turnover above ₹20 crore or multi-state operations
  • AYUSH License — For Ayurvedic, Siddha, or Unani product manufacturing
  • IEC (Import Export Code) — For importing health supplements or raw materials
  • BIS License — Bureau of Indian Standards certification
  • State License — State-level food business licenses
{# Perpetual licenses #}

Perpetual licenses (2026 Amendment)

Under the FSSAI Licensing & Registration Amendment Regulations 2026, FSSAI licenses issued after March 2026 have perpetual validity — no renewal is required.

When adding a license, check the "Perpetual validity" option if it was issued under the new amendment. Perpetual licenses are displayed with a special badge and are excluded from expiry reminders.

{# Adding licenses #}

Adding a license

Click "Add License" on the License Renewals page and fill in:

  • License Name — A descriptive name (e.g., "FSSAI Central — Mumbai Plant")
  • License Type — Select from the supported types
  • License Number — Your official license/registration number
  • Expiry Date — When the license expires (not needed for perpetual licenses)
  • Issued Date — When the license was originally issued
  • Perpetual — Check if issued under the 2026 amendment
  • Notes — Optional notes (e.g., renewal contact, associated plant)
{# Reminders #}

Expiry reminders

RegBite's daily scheduler checks for licenses expiring within 30 days at 7:00 AM IST. If any are found, an email is sent to your configured notification email addresses listing all expiring licenses with their remaining days. Perpetual licenses are excluded from reminders.

{# Status badges #}

Status indicators

Active More than 60 days until expiry Expiring Soon 30 days or fewer until expiry Expiring 31–60 days until expiry Expired Past expiry date — renew immediately Perpetual No expiry — valid indefinitely under 2026 amendment
