{# Overview #}

What is RegBite?

RegBite is an AI-powered compliance platform built for Indian nutraceutical and health supplement companies. It automates label compliance checking against comprehensive checks across 5 regulatory frameworks: FSSAI, Legal Metrology, AYUSH, BIS, and DGFT.

Upload a product label image, and RegBite's AI extracts the data and checks it against current regulations — giving you an instant compliance score with specific remediation guidance for every failure.

{# First steps #}

Your first compliance check in 3 steps

1

Create a Product

Go to Products in the sidebar and click "Add Product." Enter your product name, select the category (Health Supplement, Ayurvedic, Dietary Supplement, etc.), and optionally add brand name and SKU.

2

Upload a Label Image

From the product detail page, click "Upload Label." Select a clear photo or scan of your product label (JPG, PNG, PDF supported). The AI will extract all text and structured data from the image.

3

View Your Compliance Report

Once extraction completes, RegBite automatically runs the full compliance check. You'll see a detailed report with your compliance score, pass/fail breakdown, and specific remediation steps for any violations.

Navigating the platform

— Dashboard Overview of all products, compliance scores, alerts, and expiring licenses
