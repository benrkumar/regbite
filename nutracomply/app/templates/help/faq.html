General

What is RegBite?

RegBite is an AI-powered FSSAI compliance platform for Indian nutraceuticals and health supplements. You upload your product label images, our AI extracts the data using Gemini Vision, and the compliance engine checks it across 5 regulatory frameworks — FSSAI, Legal Metrology, AYUSH, BIS, and DGFT.

What regulations does RegBite cover?

RegBite covers five regulatory frameworks:

FSSAI — FSS Health Supplements Regulations 2022, FSS Labelling & Display Regulations 2020 (Version VIII, Sept 2025), FSS Food Products Standards & Food Additives First Amendment 2025, FSSAI Licensing Amendment 2026
Legal Metrology — Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules 2011
AYUSH — Ayurvedic, Siddha & Unani (ASU) Drug regulations
BIS — Bureau of Indian Standards requirements for supplements
DGFT — Import compliance for foreign-origin products

How accurate is the compliance check?

RegBite uses Google Gemini Vision for label data extraction with a confidence threshold of 85%. The compliance engine then applies deterministic rule checks against the extracted data. While highly accurate, we recommend using the results as guidance alongside professional regulatory consultation, especially for complex product formulations.

Is my data secure?

Yes. Label images are processed via secure API calls to Google Gemini and are not stored permanently after extraction. Your product data and compliance results are stored in an encrypted PostgreSQL database. All connections use HTTPS/TLS encryption.

Label Analysis

What file formats are supported for label upload?

You can upload label images in JPG, JPEG, PNG, GIF, and WebP formats, as well as PDF documents. For best results, use high-resolution images with clear, readable text.

How does the AI extraction work?

RegBite uses Google Gemini Vision to analyse your label image. The AI extracts structured data including product name, ingredients, nutritional information, claims, allergen declarations, manufacturer details, FSSAI license number, batch/lot info, and more. This extracted data is then checked against the compliance engine.

What if the AI extraction is incorrect?

If the extraction confidence is below 85%, the system flags it for review. You can re-upload a clearer image or use the manual Checker tool to input data directly. You can also re-analyse any label from the product detail page.

How many labels can I check per month?

This depends on your plan:
Starter (Free) — 5 scans/month
Growth — 100 scans/month
Enterprise — Unlimited scans

Compliance Rules

How many rules does RegBite check?

RegBite checks against comprehensive compliance checks across 5 frameworks: 52 FSSAI rules, 9 Legal Metrology rules, 12 AYUSH rules, 4 FSS Additives rules, 2 BIS rules, 2 DGFT rules, and 2 FSSAI Licensing rules.

What are the severity levels?

Each rule has a severity level:
CRITICAL — Must fix immediately. Regulatory violation that could result in product recall or legal action.
HIGH — Important compliance gap. Should be resolved before market release.
MEDIUM — Recommended improvement for full compliance.
LOW — Minor suggestion or best practice.

Why are some rules skipped for my product?

RegBite uses category-based filtering. AYUSH-ASU rules only apply to Ayurvedic, Siddha, and Unani products. DGFT import rules only apply to imported products (non-Indian country of origin). If a rule doesn't apply to your product's category, it's marked as SKIPPED rather than creating a false failure.

Reports

How do I share a compliance report?

Open any report and click the "Share" button. This generates a time-limited public link that anyone can view without logging in. The link includes a unique token and expires after 30 days by default.

Can I download reports as PDF?

Yes. Each report has a "Download" button that generates a PDF version. If PDF generation is unavailable, an HTML file is provided instead. Reports can include your custom branding (set in Settings → Report Branding).

What does the compliance score mean?

The compliance score is a weighted percentage based on rule results. Critical rule failures reduce the score more heavily than low-severity issues. A score of 100% means all applicable rules passed. The report breaks down results into Pass, Fail, Warning, and Skipped categories.

Account

How do I change my password?

Go to Settings from the sidebar. Under the Password section, enter your current password and a new password with at least 10 characters, one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, and one digit. You'll receive a confirmation email when the change is successful.

How do notification emails work?

You can configure up to 5 email addresses in Settings to receive compliance alerts, regulation updates, and license expiry reminders. Alerts are sent when a product label fails compliance checks or when new regulations are detected by our daily scraping system.

What are API keys for?

API keys allow you to integrate RegBite's compliance checking into your own systems programmatically. You can create up to 5 active keys. Each key is shown only once at creation — store it securely. Keys can be revoked at any time from the Settings page.

{% endif %} {# ── Admin ── #} {% if "admin" in faq_categories %}

Admin

How do I sync compliance rules?

In the Admin Panel, go to Compliance Rules and click "Sync Rules from Seed." This loads the latest rules from the seed file, adding new rules and updating regulation sources on existing ones. Rule versioning tracks which version each rule belongs to.

How does the LLM Knowledge Base work?

The LLM KB stores regulation documents as vector embeddings for AI-powered analysis. Seeding is additive — new documents are added without removing existing ones. The KB auto-reseeds daily when the scheduler detects new regulation changes. You can also manually trigger a reseed from the Admin → Regulations KB page.
