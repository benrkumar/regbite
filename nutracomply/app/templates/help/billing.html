{# Upgrading #}

Upgrading your plan

  1. Go to Billing in the sidebar
  2. Select your desired plan (Growth monthly, Growth annual, or Enterprise)
  3. Complete payment via Razorpay (credit/debit card, UPI, net banking, or wallets)
  4. Your plan is activated immediately — new quotas take effect right away
{# Cancellation #}

Cancelling your subscription

Go to Billing and click "Cancel Subscription." Your access continues until the end of the current billing period. After that, your account reverts to the Starter (Free) plan. All your data is preserved — you can upgrade again at any time.

{# Quotas #}

Quota enforcement

Product and scan limits are enforced at upload time. If you've reached your limit, you'll see a message prompting you to upgrade. Scan limits reset on the 1st of each calendar month. Your current usage is visible on the Dashboard and Billing pages.
