About

Making label compliance simple for Indian nutraceuticals

RegBite automates FSSAI, Legal Metrology, and AYUSH compliance checks so brands can focus on building great products.

We built Regbite because we were tired of watching good products get penalised for bad information

India's nutraceutical industry is growing at 20%+ year on year. Thousands of founders are building products that genuinely help people — better protein, cleaner supplements, functional foods. And then a show-cause notice arrives because a Schedule IV ingredient limit changed three months ago and nobody noticed.

That is not a compliance failure. It is an information failure. FSSAI issues 200+ circulars per year. Reading them is a full-time job. Most founders don't have a full-time person. So they rely on a consultant who calls once a month, or they ignore the circulars altogether and hope for the best.

Regbite exists to fix that. We monitor FSSAI so you don't have to. We map every regulatory change to your specific products. We tell you what changed, which of your SKUs are affected, and exactly what to do — in plain English, in a WhatsApp message, within 24 hours of FSSAI publishing anything.
