About

Making label compliance simple for Indian nutraceuticals

RegBite automates FSSAI, Legal Metrology, and AYUSH compliance checks so brands can focus on building great products.

Why we built RegBite

India's nutraceutical market is growing at 20%+ annually, but navigating the regulatory landscape remains one of the biggest challenges for brands. FSSAI, Legal Metrology, and AYUSH each have their own labelling requirements — and they change frequently.

We built RegBite because compliance shouldn't require a legal team. Our AI reads your product labels, checks them against 64+ active rules, and tells you exactly what needs fixing — in seconds, not weeks.

Whether you're a single-product startup or managing a portfolio of 500+ SKUs, RegBite gives you the confidence that your labels meet every regulatory requirement before they reach the shelf.
