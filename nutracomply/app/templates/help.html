Help Center

How can we help?

Find answers to common questions about RegBite's compliance platform.

Getting Started

What is RegBite?+
RegBite is an AI-powered FSSAI compliance platform for Indian nutraceuticals, health supplements, and Ayurvedic products. Upload a product label image and get instant compliance analysis against 64+ regulatory rules across FSSAI, Legal Metrology, and AYUSH frameworks.
How do I get started?+
Create a free account — no credit card required. You can start checking labels immediately with 3 products and 10 scans per month on the free plan. For a quick test, try the Quick Compliance Checker on the landing page without signing up.
What file formats are supported for label upload?+
JPG, PNG, and PDF files are supported. For best results, upload a clear, well-lit photo of the full product label. Multi-panel labels (front + back) can be uploaded as separate images or a multi-page PDF.

Compliance & Rules

What regulations does RegBite check against?+
RegBite checks against three regulatory frameworks: FSSAI (labelling, nutrition, claims, ingredients), Legal Metrology (MRP, net quantity, manufacturer details), and AYUSH-ASU (Ayurvedic drug labelling, restricted ingredients). AYUSH rules only apply to Ayurvedic/ASU products.
How is the compliance score calculated?+
The compliance score uses severity-weighted scoring. CRITICAL violations carry 4x weight, HIGH 3x, MEDIUM 2x, and LOW 1x. Warnings receive 50% credit. This means a single CRITICAL failure drops your score much more than a LOW-severity issue.
How often are rules updated?+
Rules are updated whenever FSSAI, Legal Metrology, or AYUSH publish new regulations or amendments. Our regulation feed tracks all changes, and admin users can re-sync rules and re-check all products when updates arrive.
Is this legal advice?+
No. RegBite is a compliance assistance tool, not a substitute for legal advice. While our rules are sourced directly from official regulations, we recommend consulting a regulatory affairs professional for final label approval — especially for new product categories or complex claims.

Plans & Billing

Is there a free plan?+
Yes. The Free plan includes 3 products, 10 label scans per month, and FSSAI rule checks. No credit card required. See all plans.
What payment methods do you accept?+
We accept UPI, credit/debit cards, and net banking via Razorpay. All prices are in INR. Enterprise plans can be invoiced with NET-30 payment terms.
Can I cancel anytime?+
Yes. You can cancel your subscription at any time from the Billing page. Your access continues until the end of the current billing period. No cancellation fees.

Technical

Do you offer API access?+
API access is available on the Enterprise plan. You can generate API keys from the Settings page and integrate compliance checks into your existing workflows. Contact us for API documentation.
Is my data secure?+
Yes. All data is stored in encrypted PostgreSQL databases. Label images are stored securely and never shared. We use HTTPS for all connections and bcrypt for password hashing. See our Privacy Policy for details.
