Features

Everything you need for FSSAI compliance

From AI label scanning to automated rule checking — RegBite covers the full compliance lifecycle.

Core

AI-Powered Label Scanner

Upload a product label image — JPG, PNG, or PDF — and our Gemini Vision AI reads every detail directly from the label. Product name, ingredients, FSSAI number, claims, allergens, nutritional info — all extracted automatically.

Extraction results are cached so re-analysis against updated rules doesn't cost extra API tokens. Confidence scoring ensures accuracy before compliance checks run.

◎
Engine

64-Rule Compliance Engine

Every label is checked against 64 active rules spanning three regulatory frameworks. Rules are category-aware — AYUSH rules only apply to Ayurvedic/ASU products, while FSSAI and Legal Metrology rules apply to all products.

Severity-weighted scoring means failing a CRITICAL rule impacts your score far more than a LOW-severity issue. Each violation includes a specific remediation step.
