Can I say “boosts immunity” on my Vitamin C gummies?
No — “boosts immunity” is a prohibited disease-prevention claim for a health supplement. A permitted alternative is “contributes to the normal function of the immune system”, which requires substantiation on file.
Cited: FSSAI-NUTRA-CLM-001 · Schedule III, Clause 3.9 · Health Claims Regulations
Which of my SKUs are affected by the Vitamin B6 limit change?
3 of your 14 SKUs exceed the revised 10 mg/day upper limit: AshwaMax 900, DailyMulti Men and NeuroB Complex. All three need reformulation before the effective date.
Cited: FSSAI-NUTRA-ING-009 · matched against your product knowledge base

Under the hood

Retrieval first, then the answer

The agent never answers from memory alone. It retrieves the relevant source passages first, then writes an answer constrained to what it found — which is what keeps it from inventing a regulation.

Query expansion

Your plain-English question is expanded with the regulatory vocabulary that actually appears in the source text.

Retrieval

The most relevant passages are pulled from all three knowledge bases and ranked before anything is generated.

Grounded answer

The model answers only from the retrieved passages, so it cannot quietly invent a clause that does not exist.

Citation

Every answer names the rule code and source, so you can open the original and check it yourself.
