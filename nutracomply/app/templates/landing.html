Why It Matters

Non-compliance is not just a regulatory risk.
It's a business risk.

In India's fast-growing health and wellness supplement industry, a single label error can cost far more than a reprint.

₹10 Cr

3×

Minutes

₹

Financial Penalties

FSSAI can impose fines from ₹2 lakh to ₹10 crore per violation. Legal Metrology violations carry fines up to ₹25,000 per offence and can escalate to criminal prosecution.

⊠

Product Recalls & Seizures

A non-compliant label can trigger recall orders across your entire distribution chain. The cost — logistics, destroyed stock, retailer compensation — far exceeds the original penalty.

⊘

Licence Suspension

For AYUSH products, a labelling violation can result in suspension of your Ayurvedic Drug Licence, halting manufacture and sales until the licence is reinstated.

⊗

Market Access Loss

Amazon, Flipkart Health, and pharmacy chains conduct their own label audits. Non-compliant products are delisted without notice — often permanently.

◎

Brand & Consumer Trust

In a category built on health promises, a public compliance failure — regulatory notice, media coverage, or consumer complaint — permanently damages brand credibility.

↗

Export Barriers

Domestic non-compliance creates a negative paper trail that complicates future regulatory approvals in the UAE, US, and EU markets.

Getting ahead of compliance now is materially cheaper than reacting to a notice later. The question is not whether your labels will be checked — it is whether you find the issues first, or a regulator does.

Regulation Coverage

Five frameworks, one platform

RegBite covers the five major regulatory frameworks that apply to India's health, wellness, and Ayurvedic product labels.

FSSAI

Food Safety & Standards

Health Supplements & Nutraceuticals Regulations 2022, Labelling & Display Regulations 2020, Advertisement & Claims Regulations 2018. Covers product type declaration, FSSAI license, nutritional info, ingredient restrictions, and prohibited claims.

43 active rules · 14 banned ingredients tracked
LEGAL METROLOGY

Packaged Commodities Rules

Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules 2011 and amendments. Covers MRP declaration, net quantity in metric units, manufacturer details, bilingual requirements, consumer care info, and country of origin for imports.

9 active rules · Applies to all packaged products
AYUSH

Ayurvedic, Siddha & Unani

Drugs & Cosmetics Act 1940, Schedule T GMP Requirements, Drugs & Magic Remedies Act 1954. Covers ASU drug labelling, formulation references, Schedule E(1) restricted ingredients, and Bhasma heavy metal limits.

12 active rules · Only applies to ASU category products
