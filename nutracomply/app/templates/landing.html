Consultants charge ₹50,000–₹1,50,000 to produce what Regbite generates in 3 minutes. A full compliance gap report for every SKU in your portfolio — prioritised by penalty risk, with corrective actions in plain English.

Upload your product label. Our AI reads every element — font sizes, mandatory declarations, allergen statements, FSSAI logo placement, health claim language — and returns a red/amber/green verdict for each one. Before it goes to print.

FoSCoS Renewal Tracker

Operating with an expired FSSAI licence is a criminal offence. Regbite tracks every licence — Central, State, Registration — and sends WhatsApp + email reminders at 90, 60, 30, and 7 days before expiry. Auto-fills the renewal form from your existing data.

Health Claim Validator

Type any health claim you want to use — “boosts immunity”, “supports brain health”, “improves energy levels” — and Regbite tells you instantly whether it is permitted, needs modification, or is prohibited under FSSAI’s Health Claims Regulations.
